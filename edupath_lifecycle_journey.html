Lifecycle Redesign

🗺️ The Unified EduPath Lifecycle Journey

An end-to-end blueprint harmonizing the learner, parent, and educator experiences across six interconnected stages: Sign Up & Onboarding, Selecting Teacher, Payment & Booking, Joining Lesson, Assessment & Testing, and Results & Reporting.

End-to-End Circular Learning Loop

✦ Detailed Stage Breakdown & User Experience Blueprint

1
Sign Up & Diagnostic Onboarding
Personalized Profile Genesis

Replaces generic authentication forms with an active 3-step profile calibration wizard. Captures grade level, subject interests, and baseline aptitude.

  • Role Selection: Student, Parent, or Teacher with dedicated profile fields.
  • 5-Minute Adaptive Diagnostic: Calibrates entry difficulty (skippable).
  • Parent-Student Linkage: One-click invite code generates linked oversight.
2
Teacher Discovery & Matching
Transparent Educator Marketplace

Empowers students and parents to discover vetted instructors through rich credential cards, video intros, reviews, and interactive calendar availability.

  • Verified Badges & Ratings: Real student reviews and mastery pass rates.
  • Subject Specialties: Clear subject tags (Math, Science, English, etc.).
  • Live Slot Picker: View upcoming open lesson slots in the student's timezone.
3
Transparent Payment & Booking
Frictionless Checkout

Eliminates hidden costs with upfront pricing. Supports single session bookings, discounted monthly tutoring packages, and instant booking confirmation.

  • Flexible Tiers: Pay-per-lesson ($25–$45) or Monthly Pass (-15%).
  • One-Click Calendar Sync: Downloadable .ics and Google Calendar integration.
  • Satisfaction Guarantee: Free cancellation up to 12 hours prior to start.
4
Joining Lesson & Virtual Classroom
Interactive Learning Space

A persistent session hub with countdown timer, 1-click meeting launch, shared interactive whiteboards, curated agenda checklist, and downloadable notes.

  • Pre-Session Notification: 15m alert with direct "Enter Classroom" button.
  • Integrated Media: Video stream, digital whiteboard, and syllabus sync.
  • Post-Session Wrap: Teacher tags completion & attaches post-quiz check.
5
Assessment & Adaptive Testing
Adaptive Test Engine

Dynamic item difficulty scaling (Easy ➔ Medium ➔ Hard) based on live student performance, complemented by focus-tracking anti-cheating heuristics.

  • Contextual Micro-Checks: 5-question quizzes following live lessons.
  • Dynamic Question Calibration: Tests adapt to student mastery live.
  • Cheating Detection: Tab-switch and window-blur analytics protection.
6
Results, Analytics & AI Reporting
360° Multi-Stakeholder Intelligence

Immediate, actionable feedback tailored for all three user roles: Student (AI tutor breakdown), Teacher (curriculum heatmap), and Parent (weekly summary).

  • Student Growth: Confetti celebration, XP, and question-level AI explanations.
  • Parent AI Summaries: Natural language reports highlighting mastery areas.
  • Automated Retraining Loop: Auto-generates targeted practice drills.
