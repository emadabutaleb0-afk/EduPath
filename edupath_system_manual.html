2.1 Teacher Dashboard Hub (/teacher-dashboard)

The Teacher Dashboard provides a unified command center organized into distinct tabs:

2.2 AI Cheating Detection & Integrity (/admin/cheating-detection)

EduPath employs non-invasive heuristic tracking during student assessment sessions:

🔍 Monitored Triggers
• Tab-Switching: Records window blur events when students switch screens.
• Pacing Anomalies: Flags answers submitted in <3 seconds on hard items.
• Score Jumps: Identifies uncharacteristic performance spikes.
⚖️ Educator Actions
Instructors can review flagged sessions, view the confidence score (0–100%), inspect timeline logs, and mark flags as "Reviewed", "Cleared", or "Flagged for Retest".

3.2 AI Parent Report Summarizer (/ai/parent-reports)

EduPath translates complex psychometric quiz telemetry into natural-language parent summaries:

Report Section What It Explains Parent Benefit
Executive Summary A 3-sentence plain-English breakdown of the student’s weekly effort and achievements. Quick grasp of overall trajectory without reading raw test sheets.
Key Highlights Bullet points celebrating mastered subjects, study streaks, and high accuracy areas. Immediate positive reinforcement points for parents to praise.
Areas of Concern Specific conceptual gaps (e.g., *"Word problems involving two-step inequalities"*). Identifies exactly where the student needs guidance.
Actionable Next Steps Concrete recommendations (e.g., *"Schedule 1 tutoring session with Teacher Demo"*). Eliminates guesswork about how to support the child at home.

4.2 System Operations & Database Migration Guide

1
Database Modes
EduPath includes dual-engine persistence: Persistent Local JSON (zero-dependency local development and mock resilience) and PostgreSQL (enterprise production).
2
Switching Modes
Admins can toggle the driver in /admin/system or configure DATABASE_URL in .env. The client auto-hydrates state seamlessly via hydrateDatabaseState().
