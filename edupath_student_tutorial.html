Getting Started: Sign Up & Diagnostic Placement
/register & /test-enhanced/test-005

Setting up your profile takes less than two minutes and personalizes all lessons to your exact grade level.

📝 1. Create Your Student Profile
Enter your name, school email, and select your current Grade Level (e.g., Grade 8). This tunes all curriculum units and quiz recommendations.
⚡ 2. Take the 5-Minute Placement Test
Launch the quick adaptive diagnostic (test-005). It measures where you shine and where you can use a quick refresher.
💡 Pro Tip: Don't worry about getting every diagnostic question right! The test adapts so the platform can recommend the best starting study modules for you.

Mastering the Live Virtual Classroom
/classroom/:lessonId

15 minutes before your scheduled class, a pulsing "Enter Classroom" button appears on your dashboard.

🎥 Interactive Video Stage
High-definition video call with your teacher. Use the bottom toolbar to toggle your mic, camera, or screen share. Tap Raise Hand if you have a question!
🎨 Digital Whiteboard & Slides
Switch tabs to sketch formulas, draw geometry shapes with colored pens, or follow the teacher’s presentation slide deck in real time.
📋 Live Agenda Checklist
Track session progress as your teacher checks off lesson goals (Warm-up ➔ Core theorem ➔ Practice problems ➔ Q&A).
💾 Private Student Notes Pad
Type your personal study notes right inside the room. They auto-save locally, and you can click "Export .txt" anytime!
🚀 Lesson Wrap-Up: When class wraps up, tap "Finish & Launch Checkpoint". This launches a short 5-question quiz to lock in what you just learned!

Taking Quizzes & Adaptive Assessments
/tests & /test-enhanced/:id

EduPath tests are designed to be dynamic, fair, and focused on helping you learn rather than stressing you out.

📈 Dynamic Difficulty
Questions adjust as you go! If you get a question right, the engine presents an intermediate challenge to test deeper mastery.
⏱️ Comfortable Timers
Each test clearly shows the remaining time. Pace yourself; there is plenty of time to read every question carefully.
🔍 Question Review
Before submitting your test, you can review flagged questions, see unanswered items, and change your answers.
🛡️ Test Integrity Tip: Stay focused on the test tab while taking an assessment! Switching tabs or minimizing your window will trigger focus warnings.
