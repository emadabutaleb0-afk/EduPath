Build high-quality assessment banks manually or supercharge creation using the AI Question Generator:

Method Configuration Parameters Best Practice & Nuance
Manual Question Entry Subject, Grade (4–12), Difficulty (Easy/Medium/Hard), Weight (1.00–2.00), Options, Correct Key, and Explanation. Always provide rich explanations. The AI study tutor uses your explanations to coach students who miss the question.

Assembling Tests & Configuring the Adaptive Engine
Dashboard > Test Builder

How to configure assessments that dynamically scale difficulty based on student responses:

⚙️ Standard Fixed Tests
Select questions from your bank, specify total duration (e.g., 30 mins) and passing score (e.g., 70%). Ideal for mid-term exams and chapter reviews.
🧠 Adaptive Smart Engine
Toggle "Is Adaptive" on. The engine starts students on a medium item: consecutive correct answers elevate difficulty to hard; incorrect answers scaffold down to easy.
  • ✓ Placement Tests: Toggle isPlacementTest to generate comprehensive subject diagnostics with automated grade recommendations.
  • ✓ Publishing: Newly published tests immediately appear in enrolled students’ recommended assessment queues.

Monitoring Academic Integrity & Cheating Telemetry
/admin/cheating-detection

EduPath tracks session heuristics without invasive screen recording or intrusive biometric surveillance:

🚨 Window Blur / Tab Switch
Detects when a student navigates away from the exam tab to search for external answers.
⚡ Answer Speed Anomaly
Flags multi-paragraph comprehension questions answered in under 3.5 seconds.
📈 Uncharacteristic Score Jump
Flags a sudden jump from a 45% baseline to 100% on hard-difficulty questions.
⚖️ Triage Protocol: Review the Confidence Score (0–100%) and timeline logs. Choose "Mark as Reviewed" (mild flag), "Clear Flag" (false alarm), or "Require Retest" (blatant cheating).

🌟 Educator Checklist for Maximum Student Engagement
• Profile Bio: Keep your teacher profile up to date with grade levels taught.
• Agenda First: Always outline 3–4 session goals at the start of a lesson.
• Interactive Whiteboard: Encourage students to draw their work on canvas.
• Rich Explanations: Add step-by-step reasoning to all bank questions.
• Checkpoint Assessment: Trigger post-lesson quizzes to measure retention.
• Integrity Review: Check your Cheating Detection alerts weekly.
